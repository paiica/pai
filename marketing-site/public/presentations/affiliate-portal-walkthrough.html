What Each Number Means

Eight metrics — all calculated automatically as leads move through the funnel.

Total Earnings
Lifetime commissions — paid + approved + pending combined
Pending Commissions
Sales in the 30-day refund window — held before approval
Total Leads
Everyone who clicked your referral link and entered the funnel
Conversions
Leads who completed a purchase on any product
Conversion Rate
Conversions ÷ total leads — your funnel efficiency score
Total Clicks
Raw clicks across all your referral links combined
Invites Sent
Direct personalized invitations you've sent via the Invite tab
Active Promo Codes
Discount codes currently live and accepting redemptions

How Payouts Work

From a student's purchase to cash in your account — four clear stages.

1
Sale Made
Student purchases via your link or promo code
2
Pending — 30 days
Refund window — commission held
3
Approved
PAII confirms — queued for next payout
4
Paid Out
Transferred to bank or PayPal
Payout Schedule
Processed on the 1st of each month for all approved commissions.
Minimum Threshold
Minimum payout of $50 — smaller balances roll over to the next month.
Payment Methods
Bank transfer or PayPal — configured in Payout Settings.
