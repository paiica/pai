Questions from educators
Does recommending PAI create a conflict of interest?
No more than recommending any textbook, software tool, or supplementary resource — which professors do routinely. You are recommending a career credential, not selling a product. Disclosure of the affiliate relationship is always encouraged and we provide a simple disclosure line you can add.
Does PAI compete with my university's own certifications?
PAI is an industry credential — not an academic one. It sits alongside a degree, not in place of it. Many universities have formal institution-level partnership agreements with us precisely because the credentials complement their academic programs.
What if my university has a policy about affiliate income?
Some institutions require disclosure of external income. PAI provides documentation for any HR or compliance disclosure you may need. We can also set up payouts to a personal account outside your institutional arrangements.
Can my institution sign up for a bulk student discount?
Yes. PAI offers institution-level agreements for universities that want to provide PAI certification to all students at a discounted rate. Contact our partnerships team to explore group pricing and co-branded programs.
How long does the affiliate setup take?
Under 15 minutes. Fill out the educator application, wait 1–2 days for approval, receive your link and materials package, and you're ready to share. No technical integration needed.
Can I track which students enrolled through my link?
Yes. Your dashboard shows enrollment count, date, and certification level — though not the individual student's name (privacy policy). You'll see total conversions and earnings in real time.
