Staying current — continuing education

Certification isn't a one-time event. AI moves fast — your credential is designed to move with it.

  • Certifications are valid for 2 years from the date of issue
  • Renewal is earned through Professional Development Units (PDUs)
  • PDUs are earned by completing continuing education activities — additional courses, updated modules, and approved learning hours
  • Renewal opens in the months leading up to your expiry date, from inside your student portal
  • Staying current keeps your public verification page and LinkedIn badge active and accurate
2-Year Cycle
Every PAI certification runs on a 2-year validity cycle — long enough to be meaningful, short enough to stay relevant.
PDUs, Not a Retest
Renewal is about staying current, not re-proving what you already demonstrated — no need to resit the full exam.
Track It in Your Portal
Your renewal window and PDU progress are visible in your student portal ahead of your expiry date, so you can plan renewal activities early.

Common questions
Do I need a computer science degree?
No. Pre-Certificate and Foundation levels require no prior technical background. For Advanced levels, professional experience in a technical or analytical role is helpful but a degree is not mandatory.
How long do I have to complete the course?
You typically have up to a year from registering to complete the prep course and book/sit your exam. If that window passes without booking or sitting, your registration lapses and you'd need to register (and pay) again.
What happens if I don't pass the exam?
One retake is available for a separate fee, drawing a different set of questions from the exam bank. You typically have about two months to purchase and use it — if that window passes, the retake lapses and you'd need to register again.
Is the certification internationally recognized?
Yes. PAI certifications are recognized by employers in 40+ countries. They are vendor-neutral, applying across all AI platforms, industries, and sectors.
How do I maintain my certification?
Certifications are valid for 2 years. Renewal requires completing continuing education credits — ensuring you stay current in the fast-moving AI landscape.
Can I start right away?
Yes — applications are open 24/7 and reviewed within 3–5 business days. You can begin your prep course the moment your application is approved.
